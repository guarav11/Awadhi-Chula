About Us

Welcome to Awadhi Chulha

Awadhi Chulha brings the authentic flavors of Awadhi cuisine to your table. Originating from Lucknow, Awadhi cuisine is renowned for its rich, aromatic dishes that blend Mughal influences with local ingredients, creating a culinary experience that's both indulgent and flavorful.

Our menu features traditional Awadhi specialties like the famous Dum Biryani, succulent kebabs, and delicate kormas, all prepared with the finest spices and cooked in traditional methods to preserve the essence of this royal cuisine. Experience the art of Awadhi cooking at Awadhi Chulha.
